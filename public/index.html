RecountNYC

Prioritizes which count locations to re-collect first, by comparing the historical AADT trend against recent actuals.

What do these numbers mean?

Top stats

Segments in scope
How many stations match the current filters.
Older than 5 years
Share of those stations whose last real AADT reading is more than 5 years old.
Stale w/ significant unexplained deviation
Of the stale ones, the share whose recent count differs meaningfully from the historical trend and has no known cause. The closest thing here to "needs attention now."
511NY events
How many of the shown deviations line up with an active work zone or closure nearby, plus how many such events are live in NYC right now.

Filters

County
Defaults to all 5 NYC boroughs, the only scope where a recent-count comparison is possible. Other counties show AADT history only.
Min age / Min |deviation|
Hide anything younger, or closer to the historical trend, than the value you set.
Trend confidence
How much data backs the historical trend line — see below.

Table columns

Priority
Critical / Serious / Watch / Low — a relative ranking among what's currently shown, not a fixed scale. Re-sorts if you change the filters. Based on a priority score: priority score = age (yrs) × |deviation %| × (0.25 if explained, else 1)
Station
The NYSDOT AADT station ID; the small tag below it is how many distinct years of readings its trend is built from.
Age (yrs)
Years since that station's last real reading — the core "staleness" signal.
Trend confidence
High: 4+ clean readings. Low: 2–3 readings, or a projection stretched years past the last one. None: 0–1 readings. Lower confidence means the "expected AADT" below is a weaker baseline.
Expected AADT
The historical trend line, projected forward to the current year.
Recent est.
Average daily volume from NYC's own automated counters near this station. "Short count" means it's based on fewer than 7 observed days — a thinner sample.
Deviation
% difference between the recent estimate and the expected AADT: deviation % = (recent est. − expected AADT) ÷ expected AADT × 100 "Significant" means it clears the threshold. A "511:" tag means an active event nearby may explain it, which quarters how much that deviation counts toward priority.
Spatial match
The distance and street name of the NYC counter matched to this station, or why none was found. Distances can run a few hundred meters, since station locations are estimated from road-name text, not exact coordinates.

Map & detail view

Marker color
Same priority tier as the list. Position is the station's estimated location.
Known events nearby (511NY)
Active roadwork, closures, or incidents from NYSDOT's 511 feed within range of this station.
Live conditions (TomTom)
Current vs. free-flow speed and congestion, fetched live only when you open a station — never for the whole list at once.
Official NYSDOT Site Dashboard
Links out to NYSDOT's own current-year data for this station — newer than anything computed on this page.
